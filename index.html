I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Southern Methodist University, on the 2026–2027 job market. I hold a master's degree in Economics from PIMES–UFPE and a bachelor's degree in Economics from UDESC.

I am an applied econometrician working on the economics of education and health, with a secondary interest in labor markets. My research uses quasi-experimental methods to study how early-life and school-based interventions shape children's long-run cognitive and socioemotional outcomes.

Job Market Paper

Mobile Internet and Parental Time Investment in Children

Exploiting the staggered rollout of 3G/4G networks across U.S. metropolitan areas, I provide the first causal evidence on how mobile internet access affects the time parents invest in their children — and whether it displaces active engagement or frees up time for it.
